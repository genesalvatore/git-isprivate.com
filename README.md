## 🔒 The Concept

**Privacy is not a feature. Privacy is Git.**

Git enables true privacy through:

1. **Local Control** - Your machine, your data
2. **Encryption** - No third party access required
3. **BYOK** - Bring Your Own Key
4. **Sovereignty** - You own the infrastructure

No surveillance. No third parties. Just you and Git.
